Salmon and Leek Quiche

Description

This salmon and leek quiche is a savory, elegant tart featuring tender flakes of fresh or smoked salmon and buttery, soft leeks encased in a rich egg custard and a flaky pastry crust.

Ingredients

For the crust

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 stick of melted butter
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • a splash of milk

For the filling

  • 4 fresh leeks
  • 400 g fresh salmon
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • Salt and black pepper, tt

Steps:

  1. In a mixing bowl, combine flour, butter, salt, and the egg. Mix with your hands until it looks like breadcrumbs.
  2. Add a splash of milk and continue to mix. Set dough aside to rest for 15 minutes
  3. Roll out dough between two sheets of parchment paper and place into a pie tin.
  4. Slice the leeks thinly (use white and light green parts). Sauté in butter or olive oil over medium heat for about 10 minutes, until soft.
  5. Cut salmon and place in the pie shell
  6. Cover with the sauteed leeks
  7. In a mixing bowl, combine eggs, milk, and cream. Beat until uniform in color and consistency.
  8. Pour into the pie shell.
  9. Generously season with salt and pepper.
  10. Bake uncovered at 180C for 1 hour
  11. Let it sit! You'll burn your mouth if you dig in too quickly.
  12. Serve and enjoy!
