Ragout

Description

This hearty sauce blends tender, finely minced beef and savory pork, braised for hours in a rich reduction of dry wine, aromatic vegetables, and Italian tomatoes. As it simmers, the meats melt into a deeply flavorful, velvety sauce that clings perfectly to the ridges and curves of your freshly made pasta. Finished with a touch of fresh herbs and aged Parmigiano-Reggiano, every bite delivers a luxurious, melt-in-your-mouth texture that celebrates traditional, time-honored cooking.

Ingredients

  • 1.5lbs ground beef
  • 1.5lbs ground Italian Sausage
  • 1 yellow onion
  • 5 cloves garlic
  • 1 28-oz can of crushed tomatoes
  • Salt and Pepper, TT
  • Calabrian Chile, TT
  • 1/4 cup dry white wine
  • 4 portions Fresh Pasta
  • 1c freshly grated parmesan cheese
  • Fresh basil, TT

Steps:

  1. In an oiled skillet, soften onion and garlic
  2. Add both beef and sausage. Break it down into small chunks, and cook it until all of the moisture is cooked off.
  3. Once carmelization is occurring, deglaze with wine.
  4. Add crushed tomatoes, then fill the tomato can with water and add that as well.
  5. Add Calabrian Chiles, Salt, and Pepper
  6. Practice your patience. This needs to simmer for at least an hour!
  7. Once the sauce is at an appropriate consistency, throw in the pasta.
  8. Allow the pasta to be coated in sauce.
  9. Remove the skillet from the heat. Let it sit for a few minutes and set up.
  10. Add cheese and basil off heat.
  11. Serve and enjoy!
