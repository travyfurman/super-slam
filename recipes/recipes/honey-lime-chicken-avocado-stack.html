Honey Lime Chicken Avocado Stack

Description

This Honey Lime Chicken & Avocado Rice Stack is proper restaurant-quality food at home – tender caramelized chicken with creamy avocado and zesty rice, all stacked up for impressive presentation!

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breasts
  • 1/2 cup honey
  • 1/2 cup lime juice
  • 2 avocado
  • 2 cup cooked rice
  • ½ cup cilantro chopped
  • Salt and pepper, TT

Steps:

  1. Marinate the chicken breasts in honey and lime juice for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Grill the chicken until fully cooked, about 6-7 minutes per side.
  3. While the chicken is cooking, mash the avocado and mix it with the cooked rice and cilantro.
  4. Season the avocado rice with salt and pepper to taste.
  5. Once the chicken is cooked, slice it and layer it over the avocado rice.
  6. Serve immediately and enjoy!
